# Support

This is a small hobby project, so support is best-effort.

Before opening a report:

1. read the [troubleshooting guide](docs/manual/troubleshooting.md);
2. run `seestar doctor`; and
3. try one target rather than a whole-library batch.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue at
<https://github.com/ColinB-WB/photon-finish/issues> and include:

- operating system and version;
- whether you used the packaged Mac app or ran from source;
- application version or commit;
- the output of `seestar doctor`;
- the exact step and message shown;
- the selected output style; and
- the target's notes and quality record, if you are comfortable sharing them.

Do not attach a FITS or other image unless you deliberately choose to make that
capture available. Remove personal paths or other metadata you do not want to
share.

For a suspected security or privacy issue, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting it publicly.
