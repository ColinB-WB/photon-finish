# User manual

This manual is written for people who can take a Seestar photo and find the
folder it saved, but who do not want to become experts in several editing
applications.

You do not need to understand stretching curves, star masks, or background
models to use the app. The default **Showcase** style is designed to make the
decisions for you.

## The usual path

1. [Install the app and its helper tools](installation.md).
2. Copy your Seestar files to your computer.
3. [Create your first finished photo](first-photo.md).
4. Review the Recommended image and its result check.
5. If you want a different overall look, try another
   [style](styles-and-versions.md).
6. Only if you want to experiment, use the
   [optional adjustment file](fine-tuning.md).

## The one rule that matters most

Always choose an output folder separate from the folder copied from your
Seestar. The application also enforces this separation: source captures are
read-only, and all generated images and temporary work belong under the output
location.
